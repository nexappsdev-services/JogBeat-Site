Free and Pro

Pro is a one-time purchase you keep for good, with Family Sharing. It is not a subscription.

Free

  • Metronome: 160–180 steps per minute, with sound or wrist taps, even on the Lock Screen
  • Running plans: a 3-week slow-jog starter and a 4-week beginner plan
  • Segmented workouts: 1 custom workout and 2 beginner templates free
  • Apple Watch app: start a run right on your wrist
  • Lock Screen, Dynamic Island and widgets show your progress

Pro

  • All 10 library workouts and unlimited custom workouts
  • More plans: 30 minutes non-stop in 9 weeks, 150 minutes a week, advanced 300 minutes
  • Running data and trends, monthly report, year in review, CSV and PDF export
  • iCloud sync, so switching phones never loses your runs
  • No ads on the history page
