Support

Jogbeat · Slow Jogging Metronome

Having trouble? Check the questions below first. If you can't find an answer, email us.

Contact us

Email nexapps.dev@gmail.com with your question. You can also write to us from Settings → About → Contact us in the app.

If you can, include the app version (Settings → About → Version), your iPhone / Apple Watch model and OS version, and the steps or a screenshot of the problem.

Frequently asked questions

Does the beat stop when I lock the screen or switch apps?

No. During a run the beat sound and wrist taps keep going when you lock the screen or switch apps, and the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island show your progress. If you can't hear it, turn up the media volume and check that the beat sound is on in the app.

Is Pro a subscription?

No. Pro is a one-time purchase that you keep forever, and it supports Family Sharing.

How do I get Pro back on a new phone or after reinstalling?

Sign in to the App Store with the Apple Account you bought it with, open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchases.

Will I lose my runs when I change phones?

With Pro you can turn on Settings → iCloud Sync, and your runs and settings are kept in your own iCloud. Health data such as heart rate and effort is not synced to iCloud; it stays in the Health app.

Heart rate, steps or distance are missing?

On your iPhone open Settings → Privacy & Security and make sure Jogbeat has access to Health and Motion & Fitness. Outdoor distance also needs Location Services. Heart rate needs an Apple Watch or a Bluetooth heart rate strap.

Can I run with just my Apple Watch?

Yes. With the watch app installed you can start a run right on the watch, and the record is sent back to your iPhone.

How do I remove ads or turn off personalized ads?

The free version shows one banner at the bottom of the History tab, and it goes away with Pro. You can change your consent in Settings → About → Ad Privacy Options, or turn off tracking in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. To report an inappropriate ad, tap “Report an inappropriate ad” under the banner.

How do I delete my data?

Your data lives on your device: delete the runs or delete the app. For data in iCloud and the Health app, see section 8 of the privacy policy.
